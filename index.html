Privacy Policy for EverLock

Everlight Studios • Developed by Zameer

100% Offline • Zero Data Collection

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

EverLock is developed by Everlight Studios with privacy as its primary foundation. We firmly believe that your private applications and personal security credentials belong strictly to you.

The Golden Rule: EverLock does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or telemetry with anyone, anywhere.

2. Zero Internet Architecture

EverLock contains NO INTERNET PERMISSION (android.permission.INTERNET is completely absent from the application's manifest).

Because the app cannot access any Wi-Fi or cellular network, it is physically impossible for EverLock to send your PIN, locked apps list, or device identifiers to any remote server, cloud service, or third party.

3. Local Device-Only Storage

All security data created while using EverLock is kept strictly inside your device's isolated, sandboxed storage:

  • Security PIN: Stored locally as a salted cryptographic hash. It is never stored in plain text and cannot be extracted.
  • Protected Applications List: The package names of apps you choose to lock remain exclusively inside the local app database on your phone.
  • Preferences & Settings: Timeout settings, lockout thresholds, and language preferences are stored locally in Android SharedPreferences.

4. Android System Permissions Explained

To perform its app-locking functions reliably, EverLock requests only the essential local system permissions:

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Required solely to detect when a protected application is opened in the foreground. This inspection is processed 100% in local memory and is never logged or saved to disk.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to render the secure PIN/Fingerprint lock screen instantly on top of protected applications before unauthorized viewing occurs.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC): Allows you to unlock apps with your fingerprint. EverLock delegates this entirely to the Android Operating System's secure hardware. EverLock never sees, processes, or stores your biometric fingerprints.
  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS - Optional): Used solely to display an ongoing protection status alert so that Android does not kill the background security service.

5. Third-Party Libraries & Advertisements

EverLock contains ZERO advertisements and NO third-party tracking SDKs.

We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, or any advertising network. Your phone resources are preserved, and your behavior is completely private.

6. Children's Privacy

EverLock does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for all audiences.
